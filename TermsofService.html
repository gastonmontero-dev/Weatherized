What the app does

Weatherized suggests what to wear based on a weather forecast and the wardrobe you describe to it. Suggestions are exactly that — suggestions. They are not safety advice, and they are not a substitute for your own judgement about conditions where you are.

Weather information

Forecasts come from Apple Weather via WeatherKit. Forecasts are predictions and are often wrong. Do not rely on this app for decisions where weather could affect your safety — check an official meteorological service.

When a forecast cannot be retrieved, the app clearly labels what it shows as a simulated forecast. Simulated forecasts are illustrative only and bear no relation to actual conditions.

Health features are not medical advice

Cycle-aware suggestions adjust clothing warmth only. They are not a contraceptive aid, not a diagnostic tool, and not medical advice. Do not use this app to make health decisions. Talk to a qualified professional.

How photos are checked

Every photo attached to a community post is screened automatically before anyone else can see it: once on your device, using Apple's Sensitive Content Analysis, and again after upload, using Google Cloud Vision. A photo that fails either check is not published, and one that fails the second check is deleted from our storage. Posts appear without their photo until the second check finishes. If the on-device check cannot run, the app refuses to attach the photo rather than posting something it could not check.

Automated screening is not perfect in either direction. It may block a photo that was fine, and it may miss something it should have caught. Blocking a photo is not an accusation; it means an automated classifier was not confident, and you are welcome to post a different photo. Screening does not replace these rules — you are responsible for what you post, and we remove anything that breaks them.
